Brief biography

A little about me

Full-Stack · AI & Automation · Mobile · Accessibility

I'm a Software Engineer (Full-Stack / AI & Automation) at Axum Technologies, Nigeria, where I've worked since 2022. I hold a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Engineering from the University of Uyo.

My work includes an OCR-driven prescription verification pipeline, an apparel customization studio and Shopify verification infrastructure. I have also worked remotely with SekondSkinSociety on an accessible React Native fitness app and with CurbcutOS on a digital accessibility audit platform. Earlier, I built web systems for the University of Uyo and Akwa Ibom State University.
